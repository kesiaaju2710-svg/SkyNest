Help and advice

Answers to common questions about our packages.

Which package should I choose?

Choose Premium Stay for your own studio, Standard Stay for a private bedroom at a lower cost, or Short-Term Stay if you need a room for 1 to 4 weeks.

Can I stay for a long time?

Yes. Premium and Standard Stay suit long stays, and we can arrange a custom package for long-term bookings.

Do you help with accessibility needs?

Yes. Tell us what you need on the contact page and we will arrange a custom package.

Are utilities and internet included?

Utilities are included in every package. Premium and Standard Stay also include high-speed internet.

How do I book?

Send us an enquiry on the contact page. We will reply with availability and the next steps.
